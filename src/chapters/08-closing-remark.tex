\section{Closing remark}

We have proved and formally verified a simultaneous identities
theorem for the golden ratio: a single $\golden$ satisfies
simultaneously twelve identities, inclusions, and identifications
across five canonical structures---the complex rotor of Euler's
identity, the real trigonometric line, the real-multiplicative line,
the arithmetic of Mersenne modulo~20, and the Galois group of the
cyclotomic extension $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{20})/\mathbb{Q}$.  The central
clause is the universal identity $3\,\golden^{\sigma(p)}=2^{p}$, a
mediation between the binary base of Mersenne arithmetic, the ternary
base $3=M_{2}$, and the golden base of pentagonal--cyclotomic
geometry through the bridge constant $\lambda_{\log}=\log_{\golden}2$.
The 52 currently known Mersenne prime exponents fit into this
structure as a distinguished integer sublattice of
$\golden^{\mathbb{Z}\lambda_{\log}-\log_{\golden} 3}$, and every pair
of Mersenne numbers admits an exact golden ratio.
