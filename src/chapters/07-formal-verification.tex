\section{Formal verification in Lean 4}

The entire argument is formalised in a single self-contained file,
\texttt{golden\_mersenne.lean} (1599~lines, importing
only Mathlib, and including the Fibonacci splitting of~\Cref{sec:fibonacci}
and its consequences), built against Mathlib4 (Lean toolchain~\texttt{v4.28.0}, commit
\texttt{8f9d9cff6bd728b17a24e163c9402775d9e6a365}).  Gelfond--Schneider
is not present at this revision and enters as the explicit axiom
recorded below.  \Cref{tab:lean,tab:lean-b} list every Lean identifier
with its verification status, grouped by role.

The external assumption set reduces to two items: (i)~the
Gelfond--Schneider theorem, declared as
\lean{transcendental\_cpow\_of\_isAlgebraic\_of\_irrational}, from which
the transcendence of $\lambda_{\log}$ over~$\mathbb{Q}$ is \emph{derived}
(\lean{lambda\_log\_transcendental}, \Cref{rem:transcendence}); and
(ii)~44 \texttt{axiom} declarations
$M_{61},\ldots,M_{82\,589\,933}\;\text{prime}$ (Lucas--Lehmer, GIMPS).
The golden relation $\golden^{2}=\golden+1$ and the irrationality of
$\lambda_{\log}$ are proved, not assumed. Gelfond--Schneider was
recently formalised in Lean~4~\cite{karatarakis-wiedijk} and is
being integrated into Mathlib; upon its landing the first axiom is
discharged. No \texttt{sorry} appears in any proof.
